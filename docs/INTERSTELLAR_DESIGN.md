# Ashvane: the living observatory

Decision sheet, 13 October 2026. Research window: 22:00:54–22:06:03 UTC.

**Subject.** An owner-operated media business observatory: four independent production
systems, their actual output, and a local maintenance intelligence with inspectable work.

**Ground.** Gravitational lens, accretion disk, flight instruments, signal paths, production
queues, evidence and receipts. Ash is raw material; a vane finds direction. Ashvane turns
raw signals into directed work. This is the brand idea, without renaming the four businesses.

**Palette.** Vacuum #05070D; hull #0E1523; titanium #273449; stellar ink #F0F4FA;
telemetry #9FADC2; ion amber #F1BC7A. The neutral is blue biased. Cyan #79DCCA marks
fresh evidence, red #FF8E88 faults. Each business retains its own restrained signal color.

**Type.** Locally bundled Space Grotesk for Ashvane's navigation/headlines: its mechanical
details fit a directional instrument. IBM Plex Sans for long settings/readability; Plex
Mono for timestamps and measurements. Fonts retain their SIL Open Font License files.
Native phone uses the same faces where bundled, system fallback during loading.

**Space.** Component rhythm 6/10/14/20px; region rhythm 28/40/64px. Dense numerical rails
remain compact; editing and reading surfaces have breathing room. 3400px monitors get a
real three-column Core workspace, not stretched paragraphs. Phones use a single column.

**Shape.** Primary panels have a 14px radius and tonal separation. Small controls 8px.
Shadows belong to the luminous 3D signature, not every card. Borders indicate focus or
section limits. Forms have visible labels and remain familiar.

**Motion.** Three purposes: one launch reveal, one business-crossing transition, one live
Core scene. Ambient 3D pauses when hidden; camera and quality are bounded. Reduced-motion
users get static scenes and immediate navigation. No false pulses suggesting completed
agent work. Loading, disabled, error and retry states precede ornament.

**Signature.** An interactive gravitational Core: an amber photon ring encloses a dark
center, connected agent constellations orbit on distinct paths, real completed actions
travel along links, and selecting an agent opens its task/evidence inspector. The frame
includes a readable operational flow and blockers. All operational claims come from data.

Critique: a generic space landing page would fill the entire page with rotating planets,
gradient text and marketing claims. Here effects are concentrated in Core and transitions;
the working studios are precise instruments with a consistent brand frame. Avoid adding
new libraries that reproduce a capability already present in Three.js or native animation.

Selected tooling: existing vendored Three.js with matching upstream addon postprocessing,
original shader/vector art, native browser animation, existing React Native/Expo components,
and actual browser visual inspection. React Flow/Cytoscape/Rive/LangGraph are researched
alternatives, not a reason to replace working architecture wholesale. MCP Registry and
Inspector inform controlled extension discovery/testing; they do not make arbitrary tools
trusted. Details and evidence belong in the research ledger.
