# Ashvane public presentation

Subject: explain an owner-operated local media company to a prospective technical
partner, without presenting experiments as production outcomes.

Ground: an observatory reads signals, directs four production systems, and keeps
evidence. Original vector orbits express that relationship; they are not screenshots,
live activity or a depiction of neural computation.

Palette: vacuum `#05070D` for the page, hull `#0E1523` for structural surfaces,
stellar ink `#F0F4FA` for reading, telemetry `#9FADC2` for secondary labels,
ion amber `#F1BC7A` for the primary path, fresh evidence `#79DCCA` for verified
states. Neutrals are blue biased. This is the owner's approved Interstellar palette,
applied locally without changing an installed skill or its profiles.

Type: bundled Space Grotesk for identity and headings; bundled IBM Plex Sans for
body text and technical detail. SVGs retain a system sans-serif fallback for GitHub.
The briefing uses local fonts and no tracking or external font request.

Space: 10/14/20px within components; 40/64/96px between editorial regions. Long
reading stays narrow. Diagrams have their own wide region and a prose equivalent.

Shape: 14px corners and tonal separation. Borders delimit information, without
repeated shadows. Controls have visible focus and adequate touch targets.

Motion: none. The company briefing must remain readable as a static document,
on phones, in screenshots, and in print.

Signature: an original inclined photon ring with four outward production signals.
It appears in the hero; architecture and Core figures prioritize plain labeled paths.

Critique: the same styling on a generic AI landing page would put glowing objects
and promises above every feature. Here the ring is concentrated in one original
illustration, status labels disclose evidence, and the document explains boundaries.
No stock art, invented metrics, customer logos or simulated product screenshots.

Diagram semantics: architecture shows owner interfaces, four independent workspaces,
shared local infrastructure and authorized external destinations. Core uses ordered
repair gates plus an explicit hold path and rollback, rather than a decorative agent
network. All labels remain meaningful without color or animation.
