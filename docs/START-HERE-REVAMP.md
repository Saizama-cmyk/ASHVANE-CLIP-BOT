# Your updated Ashvane

Open **START-REVAMP.bat** in the BURN-IN folder. It starts the separate revamped Windows build when present, otherwise the updated local source. Your existing profiles, account settings and clips stay in Ashvane's usual data folder. Use the isolated preview when you only want to inspect the screens without running capture or publishing.

The app now opens in **Clips**, with a source bin beside the actual video preview. Select a clip, watch it inline, then open its full review or **Edit in Studio**. **Today** provides the compact operational overview. Open **Setup** when a prerequisite or account needs attention. The app supports YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Discord; each platform still needs its own usable connection and settings.

**Clips** shows why a moment was accepted or held. The viewer assessment checks the opening, context, payoff and pacing against timestamped frames and the transcript. Everyday jokes, personal exchanges and gaming stories can all qualify when the payoff is observable. The finished-video review checks the actual rendered cut, including its crop, captions and hook. Missing evidence, failed checks or an unavailable reviewer block automatic upload when review is enabled. **Edit in Studio** changes the cut and triggers another finished-video review. Review switches and thresholds are under Settings. [The Shorts study](SHORTS-STUDY.md) explains the sample and resulting editorial changes.

**Publish all scheduled now** is at the top of Clips. It sends the scheduled queue to enabled, connected platforms immediately, skipping the schedule and daily limits while retaining quality checks. Progress and each clip’s platform results stay on the page. A repeat click reuses the active batch; new clips wait for a later batch. Missing media and failed uploads show their actual reasons.

**Research** collects linked evidence in the background. The starter feeds cover game news; change the feeds and audience in Settings → Research to match the channel you want to grow. Choose sources, enter a topic and request a draft. A local director, scriptwriter and evidence judge create a spoken script and separate copy for all five platforms. You can inspect sources, quotations and unsupported claims before copying the saved text. Research produces draft scripts and posts; it does not yet render original narrated videos or publish research drafts.

The desktop and phone share the same desk, research and clip review information. The phone needs the PC running, Phone remote enabled, its address and your profile password. The rebuilt phone bundles are verified, but a new signed or sideloadable installation package still needs the native build workflow described in [the phone instructions](../phone/README.md).

Start with a focused audience and a few worthwhile posts. Use the real platform results in Growth to decide what to make next. The new gates help filter weak work; AI opinions are still fallible and revenue is not guaranteed. A small set of live checks does not establish human-level judgment or predict earnings.
